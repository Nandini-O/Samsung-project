Transparent Suitability Scoring (0–100)

Algorithm Weights
$$\text{Suitability Score} = 0.30 \cdot S_{\text{Goal}} + 0.25 \cdot S_{\text{Balance}} + 0.20 \cdot S_{\text{Taste}} + 0.15 \cdot S_{\text{Time}} + 0.10 \cdot S_{\text{Budget}}$$
30% Goal Alignment: Satiety and caloric density for weight loss; amino acid / protein density for muscle gain; low glycemic load and fiber for diabetes; sodium mitigation for hypertension.
25% Macro & Micro Balance: Protein, complex fiber, and presence of micronutrients (calcium, iron, vitamin C, folate) with penalties for high saturated fat.
20% Content & Flavor Preference: TF-IDF vectorization and cosine similarity over 5,381 Indian and regional recipe ingredient tokens.
15% Time & Practical Feasibility: Prep and cooking time scored against user's available time constraints.
10% Economic Budget Fit: Everyday household staples versus luxury or specialty ingredients matching user budget level.
Mandatory Safety Override Policy

Safety Filters Take Precedence: Any recipe with confirmed allergens (gluten, dairy, nuts, soy, seafood, eggs, sesame, mustard) or dietary violations (meat in vegetarian, animal products in vegan, root vegetables in Jain) is strictly disqualified (Score = 0) before any ranking occurs. A high nutritional score can never override an allergy or dietary filter.

Educational Lifestyle Guidance

All suitability scores, recipe suggestions, meal plans, and substitutions are algorithmic lifestyle suggestions for educational purposes only. They are not clinical diets or medical prescriptions. Consult a licensed physician or registered dietitian before making significant nutritional changes.
